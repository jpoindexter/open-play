## Scope and constraints

Jason requested a credible DJ software brand in the category of Serato, Traktor, and Rekordbox, with a cheap domain around $17–$40 and no premium-domain spending.

This ledger contains **all 40 candidate names and all 93 distinct domain strings actually submitted** during this session. Four Namecheap batches contained 42, 30, 23, and 4 domains: 99 submissions. Batch C repeated phasora.app and phasora.org; batch D rechecked the four finalist domains without adding new names. All four finalist domains were still offered at the recorded prices in batch D.

The work used the open Namecheap tab and its Beast Mode bulk-search form. Results came from the rendered page, rather than inferred DNS availability. No domain was added to the cart or purchased. The software/repository has not been renamed.

## Initial recommendation — before the broader expansion

**Cuetra** — pronounced **cue-truh**.

A short product name with an immediate cueing association. It feels plausible on a DJ application and does not need “Open” in the public name to communicate that its license is open source.

- cuetra.app: visibly offered for registration at **$10.98 for the first year**.
- Standard non-premium .app renewal published by Namecheap: **$22.98/year**.
- cuetra.com: taken.
- Initial exact-name web screening for Cuetra surfaced no obvious competing DJ/music-software product. This is a limited search finding, not trademark clearance.
- Main tradeoff: people may need the pronunciation once, and the exact .com is taken.

Alternates: **Phasero** (phasero.app, $10.98 first year) and **Cuedrift** (cuedrift.app, $10.98; cuedrift.org, $8.48). Cuedrift is easy to say but “drift” can also suggest beat timing problems. Phasero evokes phase/FX but has a less obvious spelling.

## Price interpretation

- Available .app domains in these results showed $10.98/year, with “Retail $17.98/year.” **Retail is not renewal.** Namecheap’s dedicated .app pricing page lists a $22.98 one-year renewal.
- Available .org domains showed $8.48/year, with “Retail $14.48/year.” The dedicated .org pricing page lists a $18.98 one-year renewal.
- Premium .com listings explicitly displayed a much larger purchase amount and $18.48/year renewal. Their initial prices exceed the budget.
- Prices are snapshots, before any applicable tax and mandatory fees. Namecheap states that a $0.20 ICANN fee applies to some domains. Final checkout amounts were not checked.
- The .app and .org renewal rates above are published standard extension rates, not a completed checkout quote for every candidate.

## Naming method and boundaries

Used Volume Generation (Glass, as attributed in the installed ideation method) to explore candidates, then filtered for pronunciation, spelling, DJ relevance, distinctiveness, existing-use evidence, and price. This is a record of the candidates actually checked; it does not claim a completed 100-name naming sprint.

Serato, Traktor, Rekordbox, VirtualDJ, Mixxx, and djay are reference/category names, not proposed names for this project and not domain checks in this ledger.
