## Boundaries that must survive result interpretation

- A 400 ms barcode fit beginning at release is an interval estimate. It does **not** prove the first audible sample's cursor, instantaneous correctness, or absence of brief wrong-position samples within that interval. The probes overlap; they are three declared windows, not three independent statistical trials.
- “Quick” measures active hold after Begin ACK. The harness preserves Begin issue/ACK chronology and checks the active duration ≤50 ms (`keylock-engine-cursor-harness.js:97–109`). It is not physical touch-to-audio latency or a proof that setup completed within 50 ms.
- The `same` and `different` cases use bases 2.4 and 6.2 seconds (`:7–11`). That geographic naming alone does not prove a forced processor rebase or cache miss. The recorded shadow state must establish such an additional claim if made. This does not block the declared audible-position matrix.
- `completed` means the capture/run structure finished with valid source/capture status; case acceptance is the separate `allCriteriaPassed` gate (`:139–140`). Do not describe completion alone as ten passing cases.
- The two channels of the independent control must be verified from actual raw capture after execution. The fixed graph and controlled metrics are prerequisites, not native acceptance.
- This separate official-Electron test host is not the unchanged product bundle or its main UI. The reviewed launcher uses its own diagnostic URL/partition and permissions; no host-security audit or full native product acceptance was performed here. Existing production engine/WASM behavior is not proved by reviewing the launcher.
