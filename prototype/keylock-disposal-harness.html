Keylock terminal lifecycle

Isolated, instrumented test of the frozen disposable Signalsmith variant in one shared AudioContext. All destination routes are forced to zero. Synthetic PCM is captured for measurements; no private media is read.

Separate diagnostic ports observe real processor calls, DSP calls, terminal returns and owned references. Retired nodes are deliberately reconnected to a zero-gain route to test that they return false and cannot resume. Instrumentation can affect timing. This does not replace the uninstrumented functional probe or measure garbage collection.

Capture requires64 observed consecutive processing quanta after readiness, then explicitly arms before test sound. Startup gaps are retained; every frame and sequence after arm through its final fence must remain contiguous.
