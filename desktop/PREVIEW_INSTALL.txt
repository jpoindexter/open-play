OPEN PLAY PREVIEW — Apple Silicon Mac

Drag Open Play Preview.app into Applications, then open it there.
The app creates its own Open Play Preview profile. It does not migrate or
overwrite the private Open Play Playback Test library.

Start at low speaker volume. Import two local songs you have permission to use.
Keep your original music files and independent metadata backups.

This is an early evaluation preview, not a production performance release.
Musical Sync, rare pops/stalls, matched output volume and controller/headphone
routing still have open acceptance checks. Do not rely on it for a live show.
Optional stems need a separate local service; models are not bundled.
