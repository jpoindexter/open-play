## The four main areas

| Area | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Header | Deck count, View, mixer/library visibility, Quantize, panels, recording, effects, master, devices, Help and Settings. |
| Detailed waveforms | Follow the moving music and beatgrid around the NOW line. |
| Decks and mixer | Load/play songs, adjust tempo, use cues/loops, mix levels and shape each channel’s sound. |
| Music library | Browse songs, inspect metadata and full-track previews, build crates and Prepare a set. |

The deck overview is the **whole song**. The detailed waveform is a **zoomed time window**. The library preview is a **library-row thumbnail**. Their scales are intentionally different.

## Choose your view

Open **Layout and display settings** in the header or **Settings → Layouts**. Available styles are Serato style, Traktor style, Original hybrid, Traktor flat, Serato hybrid and rekordbox style. These are Open Play arrangements, not the original vendor applications.

- **2 / 4 decks:** change visible deck count. Hidden decks can still play; pause them explicitly before closing a session.
- **Compact / Full:** changes density. Use the deck’s **Pads** control for its cue/performance modes.
- **Horizontal / Vertical:** changes detailed waveform orientation.
- **Wave size:** changes the waveform region’s size. Zoom changes the time window, a separate setting.
- **Mixer:** show or hide the mixer. Hiding it does not mute channels or reset faders.
- **Library focus:** devote more space to finding songs.

## Find a panel or setting

The **Performance panels (…)** menu opens tools such as Sampler and Library tools. **REC** opens Recording; **FX** opens effects. **Audio devices and routing** manages real audio output selection. **Settings** holds persistent display and behavior preferences.

Settings tabs remain at the top while scrolling. When a nested page opens from Settings, use **Back to Settings**. Closing a dialog returns you to the workspace.

## Read controls by deck

Deck letters/numbers identify each track’s transport and tempo. Mixer channel labels identify the audio they affect. A crossfader assignment of **A**, **B** or through determines how the bottom crossfader affects a channel; it does not change which song is loaded.

Hover a control for its tooltip. For keyboard operation, focus controls and use their supported keys; [shortcuts](/keyboard/) are suppressed in text fields and dialogs. Reset support varies by control; do not assume every switch or button resets on double-click.
