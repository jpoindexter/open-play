## No sound

1. Check the deck has loaded audio and its timer advances after Play.
2. Check that channel’s level, trim, EQ/FILTER and any active effects.
3. Check crossfader assignment and position; an A-side channel can be silent at the B end.
4. Raise master gently and check the master meter and computer output volume.
5. Open **Audio devices and routing** and confirm the actual selected output/layout. Selecting another output returns to stereo master until channel pairs are reapplied.

Headphone CUE alone is not a way to make stereo speakers play a paused deck. Computer stereo has no independent cue output.

## Same BPM but beats sound off

Match tempo **and** phase. A constant small offset can be corrected with a short incoming jog nudge. Increasing drift suggests tempo/grid mismatch or a variable-tempo section.

Keep the outgoing song MASTER. Check Simple/Smart mode and whether the incoming deck was paused when Sync was pressed. Inspect the downbeat and late-song tempo markers. Different bar numbers alone are not a failure; [beatgrid interpretation](/waveforms/) explains the marks.

## Pops, clicks or stalls

Intermittent Sync pops and four-deck waveform stalls remain open. Do not assume a later clean attempt fixes the original event.

- Note the clicked deck, song positions, playing/paused state, MASTER and Sync mode.
- Note Key Lock/key shift, loops/Reverse and any simultaneous load, analysis or grid save.
- Distinguish audio interruption, timer interruption and visual-only lag.
- If a capture exists, stop/download it and say whether the defect is present there.
- Preserve the next actual occurrence. Avoid repeated clean test loops or resets that erase context.

Keep audio low if unstable. Close applications you no longer need only after preserving their work; the existence of other apps is not proof they caused the defect.

## Missing audio or preview

Open **Files**, inspect saved/session-only status and retry local audio storage if offered. Retain/reimport the original audio where appropriate. A metadata backup cannot restore missing audio bytes.

For a missing library preview, wait for **Preparing waveform…** or use the retry action after **Preview unavailable**. If playback works but the thumbnail fails, report those observations separately.

## A control does not respond

Close dialogs, leave text inputs and confirm deck/channel focus. Check **Playback Keys Use Shift**. A focused fader may consume its own keys. Read preparation/validation errors instead of clicking repeatedly.

If Settings opens a nested page, use **Back to Settings**. Hiding a deck or mixer does not stop playback. Pause all four decks and Sampler explicitly to establish a clean stop.

## Save or recovery warning

Preserve the original files, stored library and current backup. Export current metadata before restoring another file. Do not delete the profile or clear browser storage to hide an error. A blocked recovery requires the exact warning/version and last action for diagnosis.

Use [Report a problem](/known-issues/#report-a-problem) with a privacy-reviewed screenshot and concise reproduction.
