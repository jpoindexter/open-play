## Get the source or contribute

Open Play is now [public on GitHub](https://github.com/jpoindexter/open-play) under the [MIT License](https://github.com/jpoindexter/open-play/blob/main/LICENSE). You can fork the code, run the browser workspace locally, report problems and contribute improvements. Start with [Source and contributions](/contributing/).

The latest recorded private desktop candidate is **0.1.151**. Publishing the source does not publish a desktop installer or certify audio, installation or controller support. [Current release status](/known-issues/) lists the remaining gates.

## Start mixing

Open Play mixes music stored on your Mac. Begin with two songs and computer audio; add independent headphone routing when you have suitable hardware.

1. [Make your first mix](/first-mix/) — import, load, adjust tempo and crossfade.
2. [Get to know the workspace](/workspace/) — find decks, the mixer and library previews.
3. [Save your library safely](/backup/) — understand what is stored and what a backup includes.

## When something feels wrong

Start with [troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/) for silence, timing offsets, pops, waveform stalls and missing files. Keep your original music and download any recording you need before restarting.

This manual explains the pre-release project, including advanced surfaces that are experimental or simulated. A visible control does not guarantee hardware support or complete feature parity. [Beta status and limitations](/known-issues/) explains those boundaries.

## About this guide

The interface and screenshot reference is **Open Play 0.1.89**; source-publication and release-status information was updated **13 November 2026**. Your app’s window title identifies your version. Later fixes may change behavior; earlier builds can contain issues described here as repaired.

Screenshots show the recorded reference interface with generated demo tracks, captured in a separate browser session. They contain no private music library or physical hardware photos. Select an image or **Open full size** to read small control labels.

Use the contents to browse by task or search for a control name. Each article has direct links to its sections. On a phone, use **Contents**. Your browser’s Print command provides a printable article.
