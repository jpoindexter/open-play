## Temporary Cue

While the deck is paused, press its **CUE** to set a temporary cue at the current position. Hold CUE to audition. Release to return to that cue **paused**.

To continue instead of returning, hold CUE, press **Play**, then release CUE. This latches continued playback. Loading/ejecting/stopping invalidates old cue ownership; do not carry a held audition across a replacement.

Keyboard temporary Cue is **I** on the left and **K** on the right. Click outside text/range inputs and close dialogs first. This is separate from mixer [headphone CUE](/mixer/#headphone-cue).

## Hot cues

Open the deck’s **Pads → Cues** mode. An empty pad sets a cue; an existing cue jumps to its position according to the current cue interaction. Right-click a cue for its editing controls, such as name/color where offered.

The implemented number-row shortcuts cover five cue positions per A/B deck: **1–5** left and **6–0** right. They do not provide a complete eight-pad/four-deck keyboard map. The documented cue-delete chord is **Shift + Command + number**. Prefer the visible edit controls if a system shortcut intercepts a key.

## Loops

- **IN / OUT:** define the start and end of a loop.
- **Loop length / ½ / ×2:** choose or resize the repeating beat length.
- **Save:** stores a loop in an available slot where offered.
- **Loops pads:** exposes saved-loop and related performance controls.
- **Hold roll:** momentarily repeats a section; release is part of the gesture, unlike a latched loop.

Loops need a valid nonzero duration. Check the active loop indicator and listen around the seam. Loop resize/exit with Key Lock, key shift, Reverse or Smart Sync can require preparation and has mode-specific limitations.

## Quantize and Slip

**Q / Quantize** affects supported cue/loop timing against the grid. It cannot repair a wrong downbeat or BPM estimate. **Slip** is a performance mode; do not assume every advanced operation has identical behavior to another DJ app.

## Other pad modes

The deck offers Stems, Pitch and Slicer surfaces in addition to Cues and Loops. These have their own preparation, held-key and mode rules. Read [Advanced features](/advanced/) before relying on them in a set. Switching a view is not a reason to assume playback has stopped; pause explicitly when finishing.
